The following is the email template sent to candidate creators during the recruitment phase of the data collection process as described in Section \ref{sec:protocol}.

\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.4pt}

\noindent\textbf{Subject:} Invitation to Participate in a TikTok Engagement Prediction Study [DLSU Thesis]

\noindent Dear \texttt{@\{handle\}},

Warm Lasallian Greetings!

We are a group of BS Computer Science students from the College of Computer Studies at De La Salle University, currently conducting our thesis under the supervision of Dr.\ Brianne Paul Samson. We are writing to invite you to participate in our research study.

\noindent\textbf{About the Study}

Our research, entitled \textit{``To Predict Is To Believe: Integrating Content, Context, and Creator Features For Pre-Publication Short-Form Video Engagement Prediction,''} aims to investigate how combining video content, creator-related, and contextual factors can improve the prediction of viewer engagement for short-form videos before they are posted. This study involves the creation of a dataset of TikTok analytics donated by Filipino micro-creators, which will be used to train and evaluate an engagement prediction model.

\noindent\textbf{Why You Were Selected}

You are receiving this invitation because your TikTok account meets our eligibility criteria. Specifically, we are looking for:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Active Filipino TikTok content creators;
     \item \hl{Holders of a professional TikTok account; and}
    \item Creators with a follower count of between 10,000 and 100,000.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{What Participation Invovles}

If you choose to participate, you will be asked to:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Export your analytics.} Install a Chrome extension we developed, the \textit{TikTok Analytics Exporter}, which allows you to download your video analytics and follower count history as CSV files. The extension runs entirely within your own browser session and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data externally.
    \item \textbf{Submit your data.} Upload the exported CSV files through our secure collection website, \textit{TokDrop}, using the unique access code provided below. You will also be asked to read and agree to the informed consent form before proceeding with the submission.
    \item \textbf{Enable video access.} Temporarily enable public visibility and TikTok's download permission on your posted videos so that the research team can retrieve the corresponding video files.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\textbf{Voluntary Participation}

Your participation in this study is entirely voluntary. You are under no obligation to participate, and you may withdraw your donated data at any time before the final model training by contacting us at the email address below. Declining or withdrawing will not result in any penalty or consequence.

\noindent\textbf{Privacy and Confidentiality}

We take the privacy and confidentiality of your data seriously. All identifying information (e.g., account handle, profile name, contact email) will be removed during data processing and replaced with a randomized, anonymized identifier. Submitted data will be securely stored on a private institutional server and will be retained for no more than two years following the completion of this study.
