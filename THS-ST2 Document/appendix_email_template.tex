We are a group of BS Computer Science students from the College of Computer Studies at De La Salle University, currently conducting our thesis under the supervision of Dr. Brianne Paul Samson. We are writing to invite you to participate in our research study.

\noindent\textbf{About the Study}

Our research, entitled \textit{``To Predict Is To Believe: Integrating Content, Context, and Creator Features For Pre-Publication Short-Form Video Engagement Prediction,''} aims to investigate how combining video content, creator-related, and contextual factors can improve the prediction of viewer engagement for short-form videos before they are posted. This study involves the creation of a dataset of TikTok analytics donated by Filipino micro-creators, which will be used to train and evaluate an engagement prediction model.

\noindent\textbf{Why You Were Selected}

You are receiving this invitation because your TikTok account meets our eligibility criteria. Specifically, we are looking for:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Active Filipino TikTok content creators;
     \item Holders of a professional TikTok account; and
    \item Creators with a follower count of between 10,000 and 100,000.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{What Participation Involves}

If you choose to participate, you will be asked to:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Export your analytics.} Install a Chrome extension we developed, the \textit{TikTok Analytics Exporter}, which allows you to download your video analytics and follower count history as CSV files. The extension runs entirely within your own browser session and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data externally.
    \item \textbf{Submit your data.} Upload the exported CSV files through our secure collection website, \textit{TokDrop}, using the unique access code provided below. You will also be asked to read and agree to the informed consent form before proceeding with the submission.
    \item \textbf{Enable video access.} Temporarily enable public visibility and TikTok's download permission on your posted videos so that the research team can retrieve the corresponding video files. Video retrieval is expected to take approximately one week to one month after submission.
    \item \textbf{Refer other eligible creators.} If you know another creator who may meet the study criteria, you may send us their TikTok handle or username and email address so that we can contact them about the study. Please share their contact details only if they have agreed to be contacted.
\end{enumerate}

Please note that a laptop or desktop computer is required to install and use the Chrome extension and submit the files through TokDrop.

Your participation in this study is entirely voluntary. You are under no obligation to participate, and you may withdraw your donated data at any time before the final model training by contacting us at the email address below. Declining or withdrawing will not result in any penalty or consequence.

\noindent\textbf{Participation Procedure}

The participation process consists of preparing the required analytics files and completing the four-step submission flow on TokDrop. The TokDrop submission itself takes approximately five minutes, while the complete participation process, including the analytics export, takes approximately 15 to 30 minutes.

\noindent\textbf{Preparation before using TokDrop}

A laptop or desktop computer is required to install and use the Chrome extension and submit the files through TokDrop.

Before opening TokDrop, please install and use the \textit{TikTok Analytics Exporter} Chrome extension while logged in to your own TikTok account. The extension will generate two CSV files:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item a video performance CSV containing your per-video analytics; and
    \item a follower history CSV containing your follower-count history.
\end{enumerate}

Before submitting your files, please enable public visibility and TikTok's download permission for your posted videos. Video retrieval is expected to take approximately one week to one month after submission, depending on the number and availability of the corresponding videos. These settings must remain enabled until the research team has completed this process.

\noindent\textbf{TokDrop submission flow}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Enter the access code.} Open the TokDrop link provided in this email and enter your unique access code. The code is case-insensitive, and dashes may be omitted. Select \textit{Continue} to proceed.

    \item \textbf{Read and sign the informed consent form.} Review the complete informed consent form, including the study purpose, procedures, data-handling practices, likeness clause, and withdrawal procedures. To proceed, enter your full name as a typed signature, select the consent checkbox, and click \textit{Continue}.

    \item \textbf{Upload the analytics files.} Confirm or edit the pre-filled TikTok handle and contact email. Select the video performance CSV and follower history CSV generated by the extension. After selecting both files, click \textit{Review and submit}. Review the submission details and select \textit{Yes, submit both files} to complete the upload.

    \item \textbf{Receive the submission confirmation.} After a successful submission, TokDrop will display your reference number, TikTok handle, submission date, consent signature, file names, and row counts. These details will also be sent to your contact email. Please retain the reference number because it is required for withdrawal requests or resubmissions.
\end{enumerate}

TokDrop accepts only the two analytics CSV files. No video files or TikTok account credentials are collected through the website. After submission, the research team will use the video identifiers in the submitted analytics to retrieve the corresponding public and downloadable videos. This video-retrieval process is expected to take approximately one week to one month. The donated analytics will then be anonymized and verified for use in the study.

\noindent\textbf{Privacy and Confidentiality}

We take the privacy and confidentiality of your data seriously. All identifying information (e.g., account handle, profile name, contact email) will be removed during data processing and replaced with a randomized, anonymized identifier. Submitted data will be securely stored on a private institutional server and will be retained for no more than two years following the completion of this study.
